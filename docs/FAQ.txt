* In which court categories are Tribunals and Special Tribunals are located?
= To show filtered case types, steps, on behalf etc for tribunals, we have moved tribunals of civil nature to District Judge Court (Civil), tribunals of criminal nature to Sessions Judge Court (Criminal), tribunals of criminal nature from metropolitan area to Metropolitan Sessions Judge Court (Criminal).
