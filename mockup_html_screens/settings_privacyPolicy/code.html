1. Summary

In the offline version, we collect nothing about you. Your diary — your cases, your clients' details, your notes and your references — is stored only on your device, in encrypted form. It is never sent to us and we cannot see it.

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

You decide what leaves your phone. A backup file, a printed PDF or a message to a client leaves the device only when you choose to save, share or send it.

When the online version launches, storing your diary in the cloud becomes possible, and this policy will then also cover the account and cloud data described in section 6.
