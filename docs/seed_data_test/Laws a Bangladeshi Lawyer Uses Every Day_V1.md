# Laws a Bangladeshi Lawyer Uses Every Day

A short working list drawn from the full index at `Laws of Bangladesh (Alphabetical).md`.
Every name below is copied word-for-word from `bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd`, and each one is in force
on that site. Laws whose name the site gives in Bengali are kept in Bengali.

---

## Not on the site

These everyday laws have no entry in the site's index at all, so they are not in either file:

- The Court Fees Act, 1870
- The Arbitration Act, 2001
- The Acquisition and Requisition of Immovable Property Act, 2017
- The Money Laundering Prevention Act, 2012
- The Income-tax Act, 2023 (the site still lists its superseded predecessor,
  *The Income-tax Ordinance, 1984*, as repealed)

Also annual, so not a single fixed entry: the **Finance Act** (অর্থ আইন) passed every year
with the budget — the newest on the site is *অর্থ (২০২৫-২০২৬ অর্থ বৎসর) আইন, ২০২৬*.
